How to use it

Read-only · on your computer

💻 In Claude Desktop: just ask

Start a new chat and ask about your journal in plain English.

When did I go to Hamburg?
14 Mar 2019 · TravelFirst visit: a rainy walk along the Elbe and fish rolls at the harbour. 9 Aug 2023 · PersonalConcert night at the Elbphilharmonie, then a late dinner in St. Pauli.

Also try: "What did I write on this day in previous years?"

⚡ In Claude Code: type /dayone

Ready-made commands, or ask in plain English. Works in any folder.

> /dayone:on-this-day # memories from past years
> /dayone:search trips to Japan
> /dayone:reflect last month
> /dayone:setup # check everything works

First time? Allow the Day One tools when Claude Code asks.
